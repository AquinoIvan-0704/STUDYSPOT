About StudySpot

A community list of places that are actually good to study in.

What this is

StudySpot collects the quiet cafes, libraries and reading centers around town so students don't have to guess where to go. Every spot lists the things that actually decide whether you can work there: how many seats it has, whether there's Wi-Fi, and how noisy it gets.

How it works

Browse or search Filter spots by name or city
Suggest a spot Members send requests, admins approve them
Leave a review Rate a place so others know what to expect
Get directions Open any spot straight in Google Maps
